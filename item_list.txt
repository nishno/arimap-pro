[1] TOPBAR TELEMETRY & GLOBAL SYSTEM CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Brand / Startup Showcase Logo:
  - Display: "AgriMap V2" + Version Badge (e.g. "v14.0 (Live Hybrid Mesh)")
  - Action: Triggers Startup Showcase / Homepage presentation modal.

• Organization / Tenant Context Switcher:
  - Display: "EMPLOYEE AT [Org Name]" / "PERSONAL ACCOUNT" / "ALL TICKETS"
  - Function: Lets user switch active multi-tenant workspace context (Personal vs Organization).

• Offline Sync Queue Indicator:
  - Display: "PUSH [Count]" (e.g. "Push 304")
  - Function: Real-time counter of pending local mutations stored in IndexedDB/localStorage waiting to be committed to Cloud Firestore.

• Mesh / Local Node Status:
  - Display: "LOCAL NODE" / "CLOUD SYNC"
  - Function: Indicates status of private local edge server, LAN multicast discovery, and direct peer-to-peer data sync.

• Local Storage Cache Meter:
  - Display: Progress bar + percentage (e.g. "Local Cache: 12.4%")
  - Function: Displays browser localStorage quota usage (max 5MB safety limit).

• Quick Help & Architecture Guide Action (Tablet / Desktop / Mobile):
  - Display: Book icon / "Help & Guide"
  - Function: Direct jump to the Help & Architecture Guide (`guide`) module.

• Global Notifications Bell:
  - Display: Bell Icon with unread badge counter (e.g. "3")
  - Function: Displays operational alerts, worker check-ins, weather warnings, and urgent tasks.

• User Profile Quick Avatar & Hat Selector:
  - Display: Profile photo / Initials badge / Dropdown menu
  - Menu Items:
    * Active Workspace Name
    * My Profile (`profile`)
    * My Projects (Project Manager)
    * Go to Homepage (Startup Showcase Modal)
    * Help & Architecture Guide (`guide`)
    * Logout / Login / Register

[2] 17 PRIMARY APPLICATION MODULES & SUB-COMPONENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. DASHBOARD (`dashboard`):
   - Executive operational overview, live interactive map widget, plant statistics,
     seedling health, field weather pulse, daily news briefing, and worker attendance summaries.
   - Sub-components:
     * Daily Briefing Widget (`daily_news`)
     * Seedling & Plantation Summary (`seedling_summary`)
     * Global Stats & KPIs (`guest_stats`)
     * Interactive Farm Map Preview (`map_preview`)
     * Urgent Alerts & Task Feed (`alerts_feed`)

2. PLANNER AND REMAINDER (`tasks`):
   - Work assignments calendar, reminder notifications, recurring task scheduling,
     priority tagging, and alert management.
   - Sub-components:
     * Task Calendar & Scheduler
     * Work Assignments Dispatcher
     * Operational Reminders & Alerts
     * Priority & Status Filtering (Pending, In Progress, Completed)
     * Direct Map Feature Linking

3. MAP PLANNER (`design`):
   - High-precision GIS mapping engine, vector layer creation (cadastral boundary,
     blocks, sectors, trenches, seedling rows, irrigation lines, infrastructure, checkpoints),
     GPS tracking, and spatial layer export.
   - Sub-components:
     * Cadastral Boundary Drawing Tool
     * Plantation Blocks & Sectors Manager
     * Trench & Planting Row Geometry Generator
     * Seedling Auto-Plotter & Density Estimator
     * Drip & Spray Irrigation Polyline Generator
     * Infrastructure, Road & Checkpoint Markers
     * Spatial Layer Manager (Visibility, Ordering, Opacity)
     * Coordinate Projection & GeoJSON Import/Export

4. AGRONOMY (`agronomy`):
   - Fertigation & spraying recipes, tank blueprint configurations, multi-tank EC/pH
     formulation, execution logging, and automated stock deductions.
   - Sub-components:
     * Nutrition & Spraying Plan Creator
     * Multi-Tank EC & pH Formulation Calculator
     * Fertigation Execution Logger
     * Automatic Agro-Chemical Stock Depletion
     * Crop Nutritional Deficiency Advisor
     * Agronomy History & Batch Tracking

5. HARVEST (`harvesting`):
   - Real-time field weighing station, QR/barcode harvest loggers, pallet assembly,
     cold storage (refrigerator) transfer, quality grading, and worker piece-rate calculation.
   - Sub-components:
     * Field Weighing Station & Scale Integration
     * QR / Barcode Harvest Box Logger
     * Pallet Aggregation & Weight Consolidation
     * Cold Storage (Refrigerator) Transfer
     * Quality Grading & Defect Rejection Inspector
     * Piece-Rate Worker Compensation Logger

6. STOCK (`stock`):
   - Inventory warehouse management, stock items catalog, batch movements, supplier
     catalog, purchase orders, asset management (machinery), and automated reorder alerts.
   - Sub-components:
     * Harvest Produce Inventory (`harvest_produce`)
     * Sales & Outgoing Orders (`sales`)
     * Cold Storage / Refrigerator Inventory (`cold_storage`)
     * Post-Harvest Archive (`post_harvest_archive`)
     * Live Stock Ledger (`live_ledger`)
     * Agro-Inputs (Fertilizers & Chemicals) (`agro_inputs`)
     * Machinery & Physical Assets (`assets`)
     * General Supplies & Packaging (`supplies`)
     * Purchase Orders (`purchase_orders`)
     * Directory / Direct Cost Charges (`direct_charges`)

7. ACCOUNTS (`accounting`):
   - Financial ledger, double-entry bookkeeping, wage & payroll processing, harvest
     revenue calculations, expense tracking, and P&L financial balance reports.
   - Sub-components:
     * Master Chart of Accounts
     * General Ledger & Journal Transactions
     * Payroll Processing & Worker Wage Distribution
     * Harvest Revenue & Sales Invoicing
     * Operational Expense Tracking (OPEX)
     * Real-time Profit & Loss (P&L) Balance Sheet

8. TASKS AND WORKS (`system`):
   - Field operations management, daily logbooks, machinery utilization, spatial task
     logging directly on map polygons, and team daily shift tracking.
   - Sub-components:
     * Daily Shift Logbook
     * Spatial Task Logging (Draw Polygon on Map to log work)
     * Tractor & Machinery Utilization Tracker
     * Brigade & Worker Attendance Checklist
     * Field Operation Verification & Sign-off

9. MARKET (`marketplace`):
   - B2B agricultural trading portal, harvest listings, buyer tenders, supplier quotes,
     interactive route planner, and peer reviews.
   - Sub-components:
     * Harvest Produce Market Listings
     * Buyer Sourcing Tenders & Quotes
     * Price Bidding & Counter-Offer Negotiation
     * Interactive Route & Logistics Planner
     * Verified Merchant & Farm Ratings

10. AI AGRO ANALYST (`ai`):
    - Multi-modal agronomic intelligence: Gemini-powered soil diagnosis, plant disease
      image scanner, document comparison & analysis, satellite crop health, and market trend forecasts.
    - Sub-components:
      * Gemini Soil Chemistry & pH Diagnostics
      * Computer Vision Crop Disease & Pathogen Scanner
      * Multi-Document Agricultural Contract Comparison
      * Satellite Vegetation & NDVI Health Map
      * Market Price Forecasting & Sourcing Advisor

11. COMMUNICATOR (`community`):
    - Team hub discussion feed, direct messaging, technical agronomy forum, partner
      sharing, and operational alert broadcasts.
    - Sub-components:
      * General Agronomy Technical Forum
      * Direct 1-on-1 and Group Team Chat
      * Field Alert Broadcasts & Weather Warnings
      * Knowledge Base & Agronomic FAQ
      * Expert Consultation Feed

12. TOOLS (`tools`):
    - Administrative operations hub: Human Resources (HR) worker registry, Teams & Brigades,
      Privileges & RBAC, AI analysis suite, Team Hub, and System & Database Backups.
    - Sub-components:
      * Human Resources (HR) Master Worker Registry
      * Teams, Units & Field Brigades
      * Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Permissions Matrix
      * AI Tool Suite (Docs, Plant Disease, Soil)
      * Team Hub Discussions & Noticeboard
      * Database Backups, Restores & Local Cache Reset

13. HELP & ARCHITECTURE GUIDE (`guide`):
    - Interactive system tour, Manual Architecture inspection engine, visual module dependency
      graph, presentation generator, categorized AI analysis, and exportable PDF/JSON technical documentation.
    - Sub-components:
      * Manual Architecture Module & Component Explorer
      * Multi-Module Synthesis & Dependency Graph
      * Categorized AI Analysis Triggers (Guide, Presentation, Global, Live HUD)
      * "Other" Custom Feature Builder & Architecture Extender
      * Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) & Interactive Guides
      * Exportable Architecture Documentation (JSON / Markdown / Text)

14. EXPERIMENTAL LABS (`labs`):
    - Hardware integration testing, IoT sensor simulation (LoRaWAN, BLE), weather station feeds,
      and automated drip valve controls.
    - Sub-components:
      * IoT Hardware & Weather Station Manager
      * LoRaWAN & Bluetooth Soil Moisture Sensor Stream
      * Smart Drip Irrigation Solenoid Valve Controls
      * Sensor Diagnostic & Calibration Console

15. SYSTEM LOGS (`logs`):
    - Immutable audit logs, offline mutation inspector, data state auditor, system restore points,
      and export/import tools.
    - Sub-components:
      * Immutable System Activity Audit Trail
      * Offline Mutation Queue & Sync Inspector
      * Data State Conflict Auditor
      * Emergency System Restore & JSON Snapshot Importer

16. FOUNDERS VAULT (`founders`):
    - Multi-organization creation, enterprise registration, corporate legal documentation,
      cadastral map registration, and ownership governance.
    - Sub-components:
      * Organization / Cooperative Registration Form
      * Cadastral Map Boundary Plotter & Registry Link
      * Multi-Tenant Ownership & Governance Settings
      * Legal Tax Number & Agriculture Sector Classification

17. SUPER ADMIN (`admin`):
    - Root system oversight, global tenant isolation, cross-organization user management,
      and subscription approvals.
    - Sub-components:
      * Global Multi-Tenant User Registry
      * Enterprise Organization Deletion & Nuclear Cleanup
      * Subscription Tier Approvals & Billing Controls
      * Global Security Audit & Token Invalidation

[3] SUB-MODULE TABS & SECTION DETAILS (TOOLS MODULE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• TAB 1: HUMAN RESOURCES (HR):
  - Global Worker Registry: Master identity list of all registered employees and daily workers.
  - Identification & Digital Passport: Worker National ID, G-I-C-W unique ID, and digital ID card.
  - Payment & Wage Configuration: Per Weight (GEL/kg), Hourly, Per Tree, or Fixed Daily rate.

• TAB 2: TEAMS & UNITS:
  - Field Brigades: Grouping workers into specialized operational teams (e.g. Pruning Team, Harvesting Brigade, Irrigation Unit).
  - Unit Assignment: Assigning teams to specific plantation blocks or tractors.

• TAB 3: PRIVILEGES (RBAC):
  - Role-Based Access Control matrix: Custom toggleable permissions per role/user (View, Edit, Delete, Export, Admin).

• TAB 4: AI ANALYSIS TOOLS:
  - Document Analyzer: PDF/DOC contract and agricultural report analyzer.
  - Plant Disease Scanner: Visual diagnosis of leaf damage and pathogen symptoms.
  - Soil & Nutrient Intelligence: Soil composition and fertilizer recommendation engine.

• TAB 5: TEAM HUB:
  - Announcements, field notices, internal discussion boards, and shift handovers.

• TAB 6: SYSTEM & BACKUP:
  - Database snapshot export (JSON/ZIP), restore points, state synchronization, and emergency cache reset.

[4] ACTION BUTTONS & INTERACTIVE TOOLS (HR SUB-MODULE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• [SCAN ID]:
  - Fast QR / Barcode / NFC scanner to quickly pull up a worker's digital passport, verify identity, and check in.

• [GLOBAL SEARCH]:
  - Searches the nationwide/global worker database to find available agricultural labor by region, skill, and rating.

• [DOWNLOAD TEMPLATE]:
  - Generates a pre-formatted Excel (.xlsx) template for batch importing worker profiles.

• [IMPORT LIST]:
  - Bulk imports worker records from an Excel spreadsheet into the local and cloud database.

• [EXPORT HR]:
  - Exports the current worker registry, attendance records, and wage settings to Excel.

• [ADD DAILY WORKER ACCOUNT]:
  - Opens modal to register a new individual or seasonal worker with national ID, contact details, and default payment rate.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[5] BATCH AUTOMATION & COMPENSATION CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• BULK WAGE & PAYMENT METHOD UPDATES PANEL:
  - Selection Counter: Shows count of selected workers (e.g. "0 Selected").
  - [SELECT ALL FILTERED]: Selects all workers matching current search/filter.
  - PAYMENT METHOD Dropdown:
    * Per Weight (GEL/kg or USD/kg)
    * Hourly (GEL/hr or USD/hr)
    * Per Tree / Plant (GEL/unit)
    * Fixed Daily Rate (GEL/day)
  - PAYMENT RATE Input: Number field for setting the monetary rate.
  - [APPLY TO SELECTED]: Commits wage changes to chosen workers.
  - [APPLY TO ALL]: Applies wage calculation model across the entire visible roster.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[6] IDENTITY & ROLE ARCHITECTURE (G-I-C-W SYSTEM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• G - Global Identity: Unique digital passport per person (National ID / Global UUID).
• I - Individual / Freelancer Hat: Tools, independent machinery owner, seasonal worker.
• C - Company / Enterprise Hat: Plantation owner, Ltd., Cooperative founder.
• W - Worker Hat: Internal employee or hired daily laborer tied to a specific tenant ticket.
