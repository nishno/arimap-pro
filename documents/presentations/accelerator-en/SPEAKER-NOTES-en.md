# AgriMap Pro — Speaker notes

Accelerator pitch · 24.11.2026 · Core presentation: approximately 15 minutes

Screenshots contain fictional training data. Planned integrations are not presented as completed production features.

## 1. AgriMap Pro

Introduce the platform as a connected operational workspace. The screenshot uses fictional training records, not verified customer or revenue metrics. About 35 seconds.

## 2. Fragmented records make accountability difficult

Describe problems observed in practice. Do not claim all farms use paper or that competing systems do not exist. Loss amounts and market-wide prevalence have not been measured. About 50 seconds.

## 3. Built from day-to-day plantation experience

The founder supplied the six-year experience statement. Answer public questions using only facts approved for sharing. About 45 seconds.

## 4. Connect the steps of one working process

This diagram represents the business workflow. It does not certify universal automatic posting between every module. Inspect the resulting inventory and financial records. About 50 seconds. Badge operation is reported by the founder; hardware was not independently tested while preparing this presentation.

## 5. Harvest records you can trace

Show the actual operational interface. Do not promise universal scanner, scale or NFC compatibility. Passive NFC cards do not supply GPS themselves. The separate terminal pilot stages records only. About 55 seconds.

## 6. Workers can see what they earned

The displayed amounts are fictional training data. A login must be linked to the correct employee record. A role does not authorize access to everyone else’s payroll. About 50 seconds. The founder reports the LoRaWAN ID Tracker badge feature as operational: after starting a task and selecting a sector, movement is visible in the application. Presence or movement alone does not establish work quality or automatically determine pay. No claim is made that AI movement analysis is operational.

## 7. Built for more than one crop

Total agricultural holdings are not the paying market. Supplied market figures have not been checked against primary sources. A 1–5 hectare band cannot identify the exact number above 3 hectares. No unverified TAM or revenue forecast is printed. About 50 seconds.

## 8. Find companies, workers and offers

This is the existing announcements and plantation-discovery screen. Do not promise guaranteed employment, sales or price stabilization. The next slide introduces a separately labeled planned sharing feature. About 45 seconds.

## 9. Farmers decide what to share—and with whom

This is an interactive UI concept, not a production integration. Server authorization, verified identities and video-gateway access are required. A completion timestamp alone is not independent proof of freshness. About 60 seconds.

## 10. The value is in connected workflows

This is positioning, not an independently verified competitor comparison. Georgian and international farm-management systems exist. Avoid unique/only claims and unsupported competitor limitations. Eye2Eye informed the narrative structure, not AgriMap facts. About 45 seconds.

## 11. Free entry; paid commercial use

These are proposed prices, not achieved revenue or evidence of deployed billing. Unlimited AI needs separate cost and fair-use analysis. Local knowledge retrieval is not the same as proven model training. About 55 seconds.

## 12. From interest to active use

Do not fix the number of new staff. The rollout accounts for seasonal workload and time needed for training. About 50 seconds.

## 13. From working software to measurable growth

Screens and tests do not establish complete commercial readiness or universal integration support. Measure onboarding time, active use, corrections and willingness to pay. Do not claim unmeasured 10× speed or 100% improvement from bots. About 50 seconds.

## 14. Domain experience + technical development

Names and professional experience were supplied by the founder; English names are transliterations. Davit Sikharulidze works remotely from the UK. A team member’s banking role is personal experience and does not imply TBC Bank partnership, endorsement or investment. About 60 seconds.

## 15. Pilot, measure and repeat onboarding

This is a proposed plan, not a claim about achieved customers, revenue or retention. Validate commercial and technical targets with participating farms. About 45 seconds.

## 16. GEL 25,000 to strengthen market entry

The program grant has not been presented as awarded funding. Do not invent spending splits or headcount. Prepare the detailed budget from real costs and program eligibility. This ends the core pitch; the next three slides support questions. About 55 seconds.

## 17. From field work to payroll records

organizationId is the workspace boundary; employeeId/userId identifies the person; harvestEventId links weighings to a session. Replacing a stale whole array is not a universal safe merge. The separate terminal does not post operational payroll or stock. Appendix; outside the core timing. The badge link describes the business workflow; it does not assert an unverified database schema or automatic financial posting of every telemetry event. Do not treat badge history as payroll without review.

## 18. Share a controlled projection

This is planned architecture, not an implemented production whitelist in current Firestore rules. Display IDs must map to verified identities. Do not place authentication tokens or RTSP passwords in URLs or public documents. Client-side hiding is not access control. A timestamp is not a quality certificate. Appendix.

## 19. Cloud, LAN and offline—with clear limits

Do not present planned expansion as a completed autonomous system. LoRaWAN carries small sensor messages, not video. Live AI needs a configured server and authorization. Local knowledge lookup is not model training. The mentor video exceeded the transfer limit and the shared-chat text was unavailable; neither was analyzed. Appendix.
